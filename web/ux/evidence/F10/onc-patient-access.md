## Langkah terlihat (fakta � sumber pedoman, bukan aplikasi)

1. **Hak akses** [S1]: "Patients have a right under … HIPAA to access their health records."
2. **Portal dulu** [S2]: "The first step is to check your health care provider's online patient portal."
3. **Format & pengiriman** [S2]: format rekaman meliputi "PDFs, Word documents, or images"; pengiriman bisa via secure email portal, upload ke EHR, email pribadi, CD/USB, health app, fax, mail, pick-up.
4. **Tenggat & biaya** [S2]: penyedia punya waktu "up to 30 days" (+30 hari ekstra); biaya = "reasonable fee … only the cost of making or mailing"; "portal/health app likely will be free".
5. **Information blocking** [S3]: praktik yang menghalangi akses EHI dilarang; pengecualian di 45 CFR Part 171; ada enforcement/disincentives.
